How to Play

  • Tap letters from the jumbled word to spell out words of 3 or more letters.
  • Prefer typing? Use A–Z to pick letters, Enter to check your word, Space to shuffle the letters, and Backspace to undo.
  • Look for empty slots at the bottom. Those are hints for every valid word hiding in the jumble.
  • Score is 1 point per letter, so longer words are worth chasing.
  • Found a word already? It won't count twice.
  • Rack up more points than your opponent before time runs out to win!
